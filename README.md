# LootCollector – StepOnLegos Edition
### LootCollector for Ascension, Just Better.

A collection of improvements and quality-of-life features built on the original LootCollector addon. The goal is simple: make finding, tracking, and sharing Worldforged items easier without getting in the way of actually playing the game.

### Features

- **Improved Worldforged Filtering:** Search by equipment slot, weapon type, armor type, stats, and special effects. Includes rings, necklaces, and trinkets.
- **Worldforged Upgrade Viewer:** Hold Ctrl while hovering over an item or right-click it to view its available upgrade tiers, item levels, and tooltips. Newly discovered upgrades can also be recorded as they're encountered.
- **Rare Mob & Chest Tracking:** Discover and track rare enemies and treasure chests directly on the map, including previously reported loot.
- **Better Map Tools:** Improved map icons, rarity colors, and visibility. Zoom using the mouse wheel and pan around with the left mouse button.
- **Item & Location Sharing:** Right-click a Worldforged item on the map to link the item and its location in chat, guild chat, or trade.
- **Chat Link Integration:** Right-click a Worldforged item linked in chat to open the world map and find its recorded location.
- **Updated Interface:** Cleaner menus, improved tooltips, RGB accents, and several small visual improvements.
- **Performance Improvements:** Reworked background caching, database indexing, and map updates to help reduce stuttering and FPS drops, particularly when logging in with a large collection of discoveries.
- **Various Bug Fixes:** Plenty of smaller fixes and improvements to make the addon a little nicer to use.
